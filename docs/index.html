Store, track, and document the build.

AntiCEL is a place on your phone for the garage: service reminders, history, documents, photos, and an optional Bluetooth OBD connection. Most cars die from poor maintenance. Don’t be a victim or a suspect.

Support Questions, feedback, or problems with the app: SRKSuite@gmail.com

On your phone, not our servers

There is no account. Vehicle records, photos, documents, VIN, and OBD readings stay on the device unless you send a copy yourself. We do not collect that data.

What it covers

Garage Overview, history, documents, album, and Connect. Update mileage from the vehicle, the garage, Siri, or a Home Screen widget.
Share a vehicle Send a copy as an .anticel file. You choose whether VIN, history, documents, album, reminders, notes, and shops go with it. The original stays in your garage.
Connect Optional live OBD. Try it free for one month after AntiCEL first connects to a supported BLE adapter, then monthly, yearly, or a one-time plan. Pair from the Connect screen. Once paired, AntiCEL can keep looking for that adapter while you drive, including with the phone locked. Garage, history, documents, and album stay free.
